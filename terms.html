Purpose and eligibility

CodexNata gws is a personal Google Workspace integration maintained by Jekudy for the account owner and personally known users. There is no public registration, paid subscription or public hosting of other users' Google data.

Authorization and responsibility

Use only accounts and data you are authorized to access. The integration performs actions within the permissions you grant and the instructions you provide. Review consequential actions, including sending messages, sharing files and deleting data. Keep local credentials private and protect the computer running the integration.

Availability

The tool is provided as available, without a guarantee of uninterrupted access or error-free results. Google can change API features, limits and authorization requirements or revoke access. A long-lived connection may still require a new sign-in.

Third-party services

Google services, GitHub hosting and any connected AI assistant remain subject to their own applicable terms and policies. CodexNata gws is independent and is not an official Google or OpenAI product. Data handling is described in the privacy policy.

Stopping use

You may stop using the integration at any time and revoke its access through Google Account connections. Remove local credentials and retained task outputs when no longer needed.

Contact

The maintainer is available through the Jekudy GitHub profile and its available contact channels. Do not disclose secrets or private account data in public discussions.
