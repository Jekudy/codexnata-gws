Your Google workspace.
One personal assistant.

CodexNata gws connects a locally installed assistant to an account owner's Google services, using Google Workspace CLI and permissions granted by that owner.

01

Files & documents

Find and organize Drive files. Work with Docs, Sheets, Slides and Forms, including form responses and file activity.

02

Mail & planning

Read and manage Gmail, prepare or send messages, manage calendars, contacts and tasks, and work with available Meet features.

03

Personal automation

Create and manage Apps Script projects and deployments for tasks requested by the account owner. Availability depends on Google permissions and account capabilities.

Private use, explicit permission

This is a personal project maintained by Jekudy for known users. There is no public registration. Authorization happens with Google; this website never asks for your Google password or receives OAuth tokens.

The assistant accesses Google data to carry out the owner's instructions. Data supplied to an AI assistant can be processed by its provider. Read the privacy policy for storage, sharing and access controls.

Support
